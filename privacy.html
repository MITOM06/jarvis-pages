Privacy Policy

Jarvis accesses the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive) only to answer the owner's own requests. Data is not sold, shared with third parties for advertising, or used to train models. Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's own server and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Jarvis chỉ đọc dữ liệu Google của chính chủ sở hữu để trả lời yêu cầu của họ; không bán, không chia sẻ cho bên thứ ba.
